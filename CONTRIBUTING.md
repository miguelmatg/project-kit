# Contributing

project-kit is free to use under the [MIT license](LICENSE), but it is developed by its author
alone and **does not accept pull requests**. They are disabled for everyone except the author.

What is welcome:

- **Issues** with bug reports, ideas and improvement suggestions. Please say what you tried,
  what you expected and what happened, and include the output of `project-kit --version` and
  `project-kit doctor`.
- Using it, forking it for your own needs, and telling others about it.

If you send an idea as an issue and it fits the project, it will be planned and implemented by
the author.
